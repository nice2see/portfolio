Evidence and interpretation

Read the signal and its limits together

Frozen means fitted models and normal calibration scores are not updated during noise stress. Mean-feature PCA uses eight line-wise means of log(signal/reference) across 312 channels. Noise-aware PCA adds normal-noise augmentation. Rho is imposed same-line channel log-noise correlation, within each 78-channel stage or across all 312 channels.

An alarm is raised when the normal-calibrated rank tail is at most 0.05. This is not a fault probability, and no alarm does not confirm normality. Examples use the first evaluation parent and all repeats without selection by outcome.

Pre-noise signals retain small state, spatial and optical-gain variations. Uniform density and optical gain are confounded. Maxwell assumes a Maxwellian energy response; the EEDF variant's energy parameter is not Te. Relative self-absorption is a sensitivity study, not full radiative transport.

Five seeds share one measurement condition and are not five independent experiments. Actual fab faults, absolute ne and pressure/power/gas-ratio prediction are not established. Physics-trained SVM is not a universal winner.
